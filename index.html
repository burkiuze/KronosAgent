What Kronos Does

Built To Outlast
Every Session

01Learning loop

A Closed Learning Loop

Agent-curated memory with periodic nudges. Autonomous skill creation after complex tasks. Skills self-improve during use — every step it climbs, it keeps.

02Gateway

Lives Where You Do

Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Signal and CLI — all from a single gateway process. Voice memo transcription and cross-platform conversation continuity.

03Backends

Runs Anywhere

Local, Docker, SSH and serverless sandboxes. Run it on a $5 VPS or a GPU cluster — isolated environments that hibernate when idle and wake on demand.

04Memory

Remembers Everything

Full-text search across its own past conversations with LLM summarisation for cross-session recall, plus a persistent model of you that sharpens over time.

05Subagents

Delegates & Parallelizes

Spawn isolated subagents for parallel workstreams, and schedule natural-language cron jobs that deliver to any platform — daily reports, nightly backups, weekly audits.
