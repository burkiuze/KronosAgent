# Email drafting

1. **Goal & reader**: what should the recipient *do or know* after reading? What's your
   relationship and their context? Check memory (USER.md) for the user's preferred tone,
   signature and language.
2. **Subject**: specific and actionable — "Approve Q3 budget by Fri?" not "Budget".
3. **Structure (BLUF)**: the ask or conclusion in the first two lines → minimal context →
   details/options → clear next step with a date.
4. **Tone**: match the thread's formality; warm but brief. Remove hedging ("just", "I think
   maybe"), apologies that aren't needed, and walls of text. Bullets for >2 items.
5. **Difficult messages**: facts → impact → request; no blame; offer a path forward.
6. **Cold outreach**: personal hook (why them), one-sentence value, low-friction ask, < 120 words.

Give the draft ready to paste, plus 1–2 alternative subject lines. If the user asked you to
*send* it and a mail tool/MCP is connected, show the final text and confirm before sending.
