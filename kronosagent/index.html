The Five Hands Of Kronos

A Knight
For Every Task.

The only agent with a closed learning loop — it creates skills from experience, improves them during use, and builds a deepening model of who you are.

I

A Closed Learning Loop

Agent-curated memory with periodic nudges. Autonomous skill creation after complex tasks — and skills that self-improve during use. Every step it climbs, it keeps.

II

Lives Where You Do

Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Signal and CLI from one gateway process.

III

Runs Anywhere

Local, Docker, SSH or serverless sandboxes.

IV

Remembers Everything

Searches its own past to recall what mattered.

V

Delegates & Parallelizes

Spawns isolated subagents for parallel workstreams, and writes scripts that call tools over RPC — collapsing multi-step pipelines into single turns.
